# Credits

Accessible IPTV Client exists because IPTV players are mouse-first and slow with big playlists. This one is keyboard-first, works with screen readers, and loads large playlists and EPGs without choking.

Third-party projects it uses:

- libVLC via python-vlc (the built-in player); external players like VLC 3.0+, MPC-HC and MPV also work.
- wxPython for the window toolkit.
- pychromecast and pyatv for casting (Chromecast, AirPlay, and other receivers).
- Xtream Codes, Stalker Portal, and M3U/M3U Plus are upstream protocols and formats, not code from this project.

It casts through the same devices the companion Caster app supports.
